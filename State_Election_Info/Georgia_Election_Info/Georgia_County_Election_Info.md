# Georgia County Election Information

For each of the 159 Georgia counties: the county election supervisor and chief registrar (per the Georgia SOS County Election Offices lookup); whether a registered-voter list can be downloaded; the elections website; the historical election-results location and the record types posted for 2020-present; the open-records route; and the ballot tabulation method/voting system (uniform statewide).

Researched 2026-10-09. Machine-readable copies: `georgia_county_election_info.json` (canonical) and `georgia_county_election_info.csv`.

## Summary

- **Counties:** 159
- **Voting system:** Dominion Voting Systems (now Liberty Vote): 159 counties. One state-supplied system in every county: ImageCast X ballot-marking devices, ImageCast Precinct and ImageCast Central scanners.
- **Voter list:** No free public download in any county. The SOS sells voter lists; counties furnish them on request under the Georgia Open Records Act.
- **County results posted on county sites:** 80 counties.
- **Cast vote records posted on county sites:** Banks, Glynn, Hall, Newton, Oconee (the SOS posts redacted statewide CVRs).
- **Ballot images posted on county sites:** Hall, Rabun (every county uploads to the SOS Ballot Image Library).
- **EMS audit logs posted online:** none found.
- **Ballot transfer / chain-of-custody logs posted online:** none found.
- **Results tapes / zero reports posted online:** 0 counties.
- **Voter rosters / numbered lists posted online:** Banks, Cobb, Forsyth.
- **Post-election audit reports posted online:** 16 counties.
- **Reconciliation reports posted online:** 19 counties.
- **Open records:** 71 counties have an online request portal or request page; the rest take written Open Records Act requests by mail, email or in person.

## Notes

- Election supervisors, chief registrars, mailing addresses, phones and emails come from the Georgia SOS County Election Offices lookup (https://mvp.sos.ga.gov/s/county-election-offices), retrieved 2026-10-09 for all 159 counties. Names and addresses are as the SOS lists them (converted from upper case). The SOS lists one office address per official; physical_address is filled only when that listing includes a street address, otherwise null. phone is the SOS public phone number (the lookup page itself displays a separate internal number for some supervisors). Most counties have a combined Board of Elections and Registration, so the supervisor and registrar are often the same person.
- Each county's SOS listing has a direct link of the form https://mvp.sos.ga.gov/s/county-election-offices?page=countyelectionoffice&county=<NAME> (county name upper case, no spaces, e.g. BENHILL, JEFFDAVIS). This is the URL the lookup itself navigates to; elections_url uses it only for the 9 counties where no county elections page was found.
- All 159 counties use the same state-supplied voting system: Dominion Voting Systems (acquired in October 2025 and now marketed as Liberty Vote) Democracy Suite 5.5-A, with ImageCast X ballot-marking devices that print a paper ballot with a QR-coded vote summary, ImageCast Precinct scanners and ImageCast Central scanners for absentee ballots. Reporting in August-September 2026 says the software has not been updated since the 2019 purchase. A 2024 law banned counting votes from QR codes from July 1, 2026; SB 3EX, passed in a June 2026 special session and signed June 25, 2026, postponed that ban to January 1, 2028. No per-county equipment inventory published by the SOS was found, so tabulation.equipment is empty for every county.
- Ballot images: the SOS Ballot Image Library (https://sos.ga.gov/ballot-image-library, searchable at https://ballotimages.sos.ga.gov/list-public) holds image archives uploaded by each county. As of 2026-10-09 it lists 1,001 county uploads, the earliest from the March 12, 2024 Presidential Preference Primary. Every county uploaded images for the November 5, 2024 General Election and the June 16, 2026 General Primary Runoff, and 158 counties (all but Webster) for the May 19, 2026 General Primary. Per-county counts are in historical_results.notes.
- Cast vote records: the SOS Cast Vote Records page (https://sos.ga.gov/page/cast-vote-records) posts one statewide ZIP per election for the November 5, 2024 General, November 4, 2025 General, May 19, 2026 General Primary, June 16, 2026 Primary Runoff, July 28, 2026 CD 13 Special Election and August 25, 2026 CD 13 Special Runoff, covering the counties that took part in each election. The SOS says these CVRs are redacted and scrambled, with the Record ID column replaced by random characters. The URL with port 8443 (https://sos.ga.gov:8443/page/cast-vote-records) serves the same page; the canonical URL has no port. The SOS audit page (https://sos.ga.gov/page/elections-audit-information) posts risk-limiting audit summaries and statewide ballot-image audit comparisons from November 2024 on.
- Voter lists: no county posts a free download of its voter file. The SOS sells voter lists through its online store (county list $70, statewide $485, under pricing adopted July 15, 2024). Counties can also be asked for voter data under the Georgia Open Records Act (O.C.G.A. 50-18-70 et seq.). Several counties post numbered lists of voters or absentee voter lists for individual elections; these are recorded under voter_roster.
- records_found_2020_present lists only records found posted on the county's own sites (including dedicated county elections sites and county-specific results pages). Statewide SOS postings (ballot-image library, CVR ZIPs, results portal) are not counted there. The list is not exhaustive, and absence means 'not found'. Years come from link text, file names and upload folders. Undated items appear on sites (common on CivicPlus Document Center sites) whose file links carry no date.
- Public notices (of logic-and-accuracy testing, computation and canvassing, risk-limiting audits, recounts) are not counted as records; only the reports themselves are.
- Deep links change with site redesigns. Start from county_website or the SOS County Election Offices lookup if a link breaks.

## Record-type codes

Years are two-digit (`22-24` = 2022–2024); `undated` = items whose year could not be inferred.

| Code | Record type |
|------|-------------|
| RES | Election results (cumulative / precinct) |
| CAN | Canvass / certification of returns |
| REC | Reconciliation forms |
| HCA | Post-election audit (risk-limiting / hand count) |
| ROS | Voter rosters / numbered lists of voters |
| TAPE | Results tapes / zero & totals reports |
| HASH | Hash validation / L&A test reports |
| LOG | EMS audit / event logs |
| XFER | Ballot transfer / chain-of-custody logs |
| CVR | Cast vote records (CVR) |
| IMG | Ballot images / scanned ballots |
| VF | Full registered-voter file |
