# Texas County Election Information

For each of the 254 Texas counties: the voter registrar and whether a registered-voter list can be downloaded; the elections website; the historical election-results location and the record types posted for 2020-present; the open-records (Public Information Act) route; and the ballot tabulation method/voting system.

Researched 2026-10-03. Machine-readable copies: `texas_county_election_info.json` (canonical) and `texas_county_election_info.csv`.

## Summary

- **Counties:** 254
- **Voting systems** (SOS inventory rev. 09/01/2026): ES&S 141, Hart 113. Every county is machine-tabulated for general elections.
- **Party primary hand counts:** Calhoun, Eastland, Gillespie (Republican primaries; see notes).
- **Free full voter-list download:** Dallas, El Paso, Travis. All other counties furnish the list on request (Tex. Elec. Code 18.008).
- **Cast vote records posted online:** Bexar, Briscoe, Burnet, Clay, Cooke, Hays, Hidalgo, Moore, Parker, San Jacinto, Wichita.
- **Ballot images posted online:** Bexar, Burnet, Clay.
- **EMS audit logs posted online:** Parker.
- **Ballot transfer / chain-of-custody logs posted online:** Washington.
- **Results tapes / zero reports posted online:** 16 counties.
- **Voter rosters posted online:** 131 counties.
- **Dedicated open-records portal or page found:** 77 counties; the rest take written Public Information Act requests by mail, email, or in person.

## Notes

- Election officials, voter registrars and contact details come from the Texas SOS Election Duties and County Voter Registration Officials pages (retrieved 2026-10-03).
- Voting systems come from the SOS "Voting Systems by County" inventory revised 09/01/2026. Every county is listed with ES&S or Hart InterCivic equipment; no county uses hand counting as its adopted method for general elections.
- Party primaries may be hand-counted by decision of a county executive committee; known cases are noted under tabulation.notes.
- Voter lists: Texas does not require counties to post the voter file. County registrars furnish it on request under Tex. Elec. Code 18.008 (affidavit of non-commercial use, cost-based fee); the SOS furnishes the statewide list under 18.066. Daily early-voting rosters (Elec. Code 87.121) are posted by many counties and appear under voter_roster.
- Counties must post official results (Elec. Code 65.016), reconciliation forms (127.131(f)), polling-place reconciliations (66.005), early-voting rosters (87.121) and post-election hand-count audit results (127.201(e)). CVRs, ballot images, results tapes, and EMS logs are not required to be posted; where they are not online, request them through open records.
- records_found_2020_present lists record types detected on the county site (crawl of up to 60 pages per county plus manual browser checks for large or bot-blocked sites). It shows what is posted, not an exhaustive inventory; absence means "not found", not "does not exist". Years are inferred from file names, link text and upload paths.
- Deep links can change when counties redesign their sites; county_website and the SOS listing allow re-navigation.

## Record-type codes

Years are two-digit (`22-24` = 2022–2024); `undated` = items whose year could not be inferred.

| Code | Record type |
|------|-------------|
| RES | Election results (cumulative / precinct) |
| CAN | Canvass reports |
| REC | Reconciliation forms |
| HCA | Post-election hand-count audit |
| ROS | Voter rosters (early voting / election day) |
| TAPE | Results tapes / zero & totals reports |
| HASH | Hash validation / L&A test certifications |
| LOG | EMS audit / event logs |
| XFER | Ballot transfer / chain-of-custody logs |
| CVR | Cast vote records (CVR) |
| IMG | Ballot images / scanned ballots |
| VF | Full registered-voter file |

## County notes

- **Aransas:** Current results, prior-election archive and daily voter rosters are hosted on Box.com (actx.box.com).
- **Bastrop:** Clarity ENR holds 2012-current results; site archive holds 2006-2012.
- **Bee:** Elections History page holds official results with reconciliation from 2016 onward.
- **Bell:** Results published through Clarity ENR; no document archive found on the county elections page.
- **Brazoria:** 'Voter Lists' page posts per-election lists of voters (2023-2026). Election Results page posts canvass, preliminary/official reconciliation, polling-place reconciliation and PEHCA certifications; Historical page covers older elections back to the 1920s.
- **Calhoun:** Republican Party hand-counted its March 2026 primary ballots (county executive committee decision); other elections machine-tabulated.
- **Dallas:** Dallas County Republicans proposed hand-counting the March 2026 primary but abandoned the plan; ballots were machine-tabulated. Per-election pages under /election-results/historical/ post unofficial & official results, preliminary/final reconciliation forms, Election Day and in-person early-voting rosters (XLSX), mail-ballot return reports, turnout by vote center, and post-election hand-count audits. Full countywide voter list posted monthly as a free TXT download.
- **Denton:** Results published through Clarity ENR (votedenton.gov links to it).
- **Eastland:** Republican Party hand-counted its March 2026 primary ballots (county executive committee decision); other elections machine-tabulated.
- **El Paso:** Countywide voter file posted as XLSX/TXT (updated periodically).
- **Galveston:** Historical Elections page posts canvass and cumulative results in PDF and CSV, reconciliation reports and PEHCA certifications; Clarity ENR for election night.
- **Gillespie:** Republican Party hand-counted its March 2024 and March 2026 primary ballots (county executive committee decision); other elections machine-tabulated.
- **Glasscock:** SOS inventory lists ballot-marking devices and EMS software but no ballot scanner; tabulation method unconfirmed - contact the county.
- **Gregg:** Elections website greggcountyvotes.com has a past-results archive (2016 onward).
- **Jack:** Election results page lists official canvass, cumulative and precinct results and early-voting/Election Day rosters (per site search; site refused automated access).
- **Johnson:** Official results archive with precinct-by-precinct results.
- **Kent:** SOS inventory lists only ES&S EMS software (no scanners or ballot markers); tabulation method unconfirmed - contact the county.
- **Loving:** SOS inventory lists ballot-marking devices and EMS software but no ballot scanner; tabulation method unconfirmed - contact the county. No dedicated elections page found on the county website; contact the elections office directly.
- **Nueces:** Election History page has Cumulative Results and Precinct Results document folders.
- **Potter:** Archived results page plus an In-Person & Mail Ballot Roster page.
- **Reeves:** Also has a Voter Archive page; live results via Enhanced Voting.
- **Rockwall:** Results published through Clarity ENR.
- **Smith:** Election archive hosted in a Laserfiche public portal.
- **Somervell:** Past Election Results page lists elections through 2022.
- **Travis:** Voter registrar (Tax Assessor-Collector) ArcGIS hub posts the full registered-voter list and a voter list with voting history (2016-present) as free CSV downloads.
- **Webb:** Archived results include official and precinct-by-precinct results pages by year.
