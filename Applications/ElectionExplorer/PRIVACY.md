# Election Explorer — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 11, 2026_

Election Explorer is a desktop application published by WheelGroupTech. We
designed it to run entirely on your computer.

## Information we collect

Election Explorer does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal
information. We have no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising, or telemetry,
and the app makes no background network connections.

## Your data stays on your device

- **Voter lists and files you open** are read and processed locally, in memory,
  on your computer. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere.
- **Application settings** (such as zoom level, preferred map provider, and
  name/copy formatting) are saved locally in the Windows registry on your
  device. They are not transmitted to us or anyone else.

## Opening addresses in a map service

When you choose **"Show in Maps,"** Election Explorer opens your web browser to
the mapping provider you selected (Google Maps, Bing Maps, Apple Maps, or
OpenStreetMap) with the address you selected. This is a normal visit to that
third-party website using your browser; any information handled at that point is
governed by that provider's own privacy policy. Election Explorer itself sends
nothing to us.

## Children's privacy

Election Explorer does not knowingly collect any information from anyone,
including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
