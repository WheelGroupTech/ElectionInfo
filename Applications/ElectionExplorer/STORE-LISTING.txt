Election Explorer is a fast, private desktop tool for working with the election data counties publish: voter-registration lists, voter rosters (who has voted), and cast vote records (CVRs). Open files with millions of voters or hundreds of thousands of ballots, and browse, search, filter, tabulate, and compare them in seconds, all on your own computer.

Everything happens locally. Election Explorer has no accounts, no ads, and no telemetry, and it never uploads your files. Your data stays on your machine.

BUILT FOR REAL VOTER LISTS
Election Explorer opens the large CSV, TSV, and Excel (.xlsx) files that counties publish, even lists with millions of voters and hundreds of columns. Files load in the background so the app stays responsive, and appear in a clean two-pane grid that keeps the key columns (Voter ID, Precinct, Name, and Address) pinned in place while you scroll through the rest. Counties lay out their files differently; Election Explorer recognizes the columns by name, including the Travis, Dallas, and El Paso County, Texas, layouts, and builds a consistent Voter ID and address from them.

FIND WHAT YOU NEED
Sort by any column and apply flexible, rule-based filters to narrow a list down to exactly the voters you're looking for. Addresses are automatically normalized to a consistent format, so the same address written different ways lines up.

CHECK DATA QUALITY
Spot problems quickly: find duplicate Voter IDs, or duplicate voter records that share the same name and date of birth, including those who voted under different Voter IDs in an election's voter roster.

SEE THE BIG PICTURE
Generate instant summary reports, such as the number of voters per precinct or per address, to understand the shape of a list at a glance.

COMPARE TWO LISTS
Open two versions of a list (for example, before and after an update) and compare them by Voter ID. Election Explorer shows who's only in one file, who's unchanged, and who changed, grading name and address edits as minor or major and flagging precinct changes. A side-by-side Differences view lets you review every changed field and copy the results. Rows are paired one to one, so a voter a county lists twice is reported as a repeated Voter ID instead of throwing off the counts. Export the summary, every compared record, or the differences to CSV or TSV.

TRACK WHO HAS VOTED
Load the voter rosters a county publishes during an election, the daily lists of who voted by mail, early, on Election Day, provisionally, or by limited ballot. Election Explorer reads Travis County, Texas, roster ZIP files as published (or their Excel, CSV, and TSV files) into a Voter Roster window showing each voter's Voter ID, precinct, name, voting method, and date voted. Real rosters contain clerical errors, and Election Explorer handles them: corrected files replace the originals, misnamed duplicate files and rows that aren't voters are skipped, a sheet missing its header row is recovered, and duplicate Voter IDs are kept and flagged, with a load summary of everything it found.

The Voting Totals report counts voters per day and voting method, each voter once, split by party in primaries. Filter by method, date, or precinct, compare a roster with a later roster or with the voter list (matching names on first and last name), and export the roster to CSV or TSV.

EXPLORE CAST VOTE RECORDS
Load the cast vote records exported by ES&S, Hart InterCivic, and Dominion (now Liberty Vote) voting systems: Excel, CSV, or TSV files from ES&S, the ZIP archives of ballot files and PDF CVR Reports produced by Hart (including scanned, image-only reports, read with the OCR built into Windows), and the ZIP exports produced by Dominion's Democracy Suite. Load a Hart ZIP together with its PDF report to add each ballot's polling place, voting type, and scanning device. Combine several files from the same election, such as early voting, Election Day, and mail ballots, into a single view. Large exports, including multi-gigabyte Hart and Dominion archives, load in the background with live progress.

Every ballot record appears in a sortable grid with a column for each contest. Filter on any value, such as a precinct, a batch, or a vote in a particular race, to focus on just the ballots you care about.

TABULATE THE VOTES
Count every contest in one step: candidate totals plus write-ins, overvotes, and undervotes, with multi-seat ("vote for N") races summed correctly. Tabulate all ballots, or only the ones that match your current filter. In primaries, each party's races are counted separately, and you choose which party is listed first. Additional reports count ballot records by batch, precinct, ballot style, polling place, voting device, and voting type.

RANKED-CHOICE VOTING
For ranked-choice contests, each ranking gets its own column, and Election Explorer runs the instant runoff round by round: every candidate's votes in each round, who was eliminated, and how many ballots continued, were exhausted, or stopped at an overvoted ranking. Run it on all ballots or only the ones that match your filter, then copy or export the rounds.

WORK YOUR WAY
Copy selected rows to the clipboard, or export any list, ballot view, or report to CSV or TSV for use in other tools. Look up any address in your preferred map service (Google, Bing, Apple, or OpenStreetMap), and adjust zoom and display options to suit you. Your preferences are remembered between sessions.

RUNS EVERYWHERE WINDOWS DOES
Native builds for both Intel/AMD (x64) and ARM64 PCs, on Windows 10 and Windows 11.
