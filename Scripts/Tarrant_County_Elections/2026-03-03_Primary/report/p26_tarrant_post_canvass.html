Post-canvass audit · Tarrant County, Texas · Primary of March 3, 2026

Tarrant County March 2026 Primary: Post-Canvass Reconciliation

Independent check of the official canvass against the county's own cast vote records, scanner results tapes, voter rosters, audit reports, and a third-party reconciliation chart. All source files were published by Tarrant County Elections Administration.

Answers

Match
Do the CVRs match the official published results?
Yes, exactly. All 1,307 candidate/vote-type totals, every undervote and overvote total, and every precinct-level figure in the official Clarity detail file equal a tabulation of the 488,862 Hart CVR records (337,054 ballots). The 199 per-polling-place Election Day reports also match the CVRs on all 134,643 lines.
Exposed
Were roster entries redacted to protect ballot secrecy, and does it hold up?
The county redacted voters whose precinct had only one or two ballots of their party by that method. That rule held in the March roster reports, but the April "Redacted" full roster names the voters again. In 133 precinct/method/party groups exactly one ballot was cast and the full roster names that voter, so the public CVR shows how they voted. The county did not redact for polling-place uniqueness: 16,031 in-person ballots were the only ballot of their precinct cast at that vote center.
Small gaps
Are there more votes cast than voters?
Not countywide against poll-book check-ins, but yes in places: 21 polling sites counted more ballots than they checked in voters (largest: New Beginnings Church, Democratic Election Day, 95 ballots for 86 check-ins). The county's own state reconciliation form shows 20 more Republican mail ballots counted than accepted mail voters and 48 more Democratic early-voting ballots than early voters. The published full rosters list 221 fewer Republican and 240 fewer Democratic voters than ballots counted.
Match, gaps
Do the polling-place results tapes match the CVRs?
Every published tape with results matches the CVRs of its scanner. No vote count on any tape was found to differ from the CVR. But the published set is incomplete: 38 scanners that counted 36,544 in-person ballots have no tape with results (7 early-voting units with 28,543 ballots, 31 Election Day units with 8,001), and 2 more Republican tapes are torn off after the header. Two Election Day tapes show ballots that were later rescanned on other units, and the county filed several tapes under the wrong site or party.
Mostly
Is the "Election Reconciliation Chart – Official Totals 3-3-2026" correct?
Most cells trace correctly to their sources. Three need correction: the 11,693 mail figure is ballots sent, not counted; the Republican Election Day "Scanner Voter Tapes" figure (45,574) does not reproduce, and every tape figure leaves out ballots on scanners with no published tape; and the "Daily Count Total" of 51,065 is the report's unlabeled Verity scanner total, not check-ins minus provisionals (51,103). The CVR column includes provisional ballots, so it is not comparable to the counted columns without that adjustment. See the cell-by-cell check below.

CVRs against the official results

  • Source: six Hart Verity CVR export ZIPs (one XML per sheet) and the matching six "CVR Report" PDFs, which add polling place, scanner serial and voting type. Every CVR ID appears in both, with the same precinct.
  • Official comparison: Clarity detail.xml (181 contests: 104 Republican, 77 Democratic) by contest, choice, vote type (Absentee, Early Voting, Election) and precinct, plus undervotes and overvotes by precinct. Zero differences.
  • Second source: the county's "ED Locations Cumulative Reports" (one report per Election Day site, run 3/23/2026) match the CVRs grouped by polling place on every line: 78,139 Republican and 56,504 Democratic lines.
  • Hand-count audit: the certification shows three lines one vote above the electronic tally (Cornyn at Arlington South Service Center; Chip Roy and Phil Sorrels at Como Community Center, all Republican early voting), the same three the county's PEHCA letter attributes to tally errors. The electronic figures on the certification equal the CVR.
Report defects found along the way.
  • The ED location report file contains two reports titled "Tarrant County Plaza Building". The second (270 Republican / 218 Democratic ballots) is the "Tarrant County Precinct 1" site; its votes match that site's CVRs exactly.
  • Four Election Day sites appear in the CVR and on their own tapes only as "ED Location 201–204". Ballot counts identify them as Agape United Christian Fellowship, Alpha International Seventh-Day Adventist Church, House of Prayer & Praise Church and James Starrett Elementary School.
  • Edify Community Fellowship Church (Democratic, Election Day) has 189 CVR ballots against a Verity count of 187. The extra 2 came from a second scanner, S1902551408, which also scanned 1 Republican ballot at Dover Fellowship Hall. This is the source of the 62,058 vs 62,056 difference in the chart.
  • Some CVR Report PDF pages omit the precinct and device type (16,692 Republican EV, 4,223 Democratic EV and 2,207 Republican ED records). The XML export carries the precinct for all of them.

Poll-book check-ins by site

The Election Day cross-check report's "EPB Checkins" excludes provisional check-ins (ballots equal check-ins at sites with provisionals, site after site). Against it:

  • Election Day, Republican: 51,214 check-ins, 51,065 ballots (149 fewer ballots). 13 sites had more ballots than check-ins (17 extra), led by Bethlehem Baptist Church (174 ballots, 169 check-ins).
  • Election Day, Democratic: 62,350 check-ins, 62,058 ballots (292 fewer). 5 sites had more ballots than check-ins (13 extra), led by New Beginnings Church (95 ballots, 86 check-ins).
  • Early voting (both parties): the 40 site rows total 213,992 check-ins and 213,896 Verity ballots, which equal the CVR at every site. Three sites counted more ballots than check-ins: Tarrant County Plaza Building (+4), Sheriff's Office North Patrol Division (+1), TCC Southeast Campus (+1).
The Early Voting Audit Report's printed grand totals (358,778 check-ins, 538 provisional, 358,619 Verity) do not equal the sum of its own 40 rows (213,992 / 327 / 213,896). The rows reconcile to the CVR; the total line does not.

Results tapes against the CVRs

The three tape ZIPs hold 425 TIFF images: 57 early voting, 183 Democratic and 185 Republican Election Day. Hart Verity Scan tapes print the polling place, scanner serial, ballot counter, every contest and a ballot count by party. Each tape was read with OCR and paired to its scanner in the CVR Report PDFs. Pairing used the polling place and party first, then the printed serial and ballot counter, because OCR often misreads a serial digit. Every count was then compared with the CVR tally for that scanner and voting method.

Rescanned ballots

When ballots are rescanned at central count, the CVRs carry the rescanning unit's serial while the original tape stays in the records. Two Election Day tapes show this:

  • Dover Fellowship Hall (Republican): the tape is from S1902990209, a serial that appears nowhere in the CVR, and reports 457 ballots. The CVR spreads the site's 457 ballots across S1902994209 (283), S1903069109 (91), S1902533708 (82) and S1902551408 (1). The two middle units are early-voting scanners (Handley-Meadowbrook #2, Bagsby #3). The tape's vote counts equal the site's CVR totals.
  • The Pentecostals of Fort Worth (Democratic): the tape is from S1902663108 (188 ballots); all 188 CVRs carry S1902544208. Votes match.
  • S1902551408 also carries 2 Democratic ballots at Edify Community Fellowship Church that are not on Edify's tape (187). That is why the official Election Day count (62,058) is 2 above the cross-check report (62,056).
  • Handley-Meadowbrook #2 (S1903069109) printed 4 Republican early-voting ballots. The CVR gives that serial 5, one of them labelled "EV – Benbrook Community Center".
  • Scanner S1902552008 carries the 142 Democratic limited ballots (its tape matches) and the 73 Republican and 210 Democratic Election Day provisional ballots, which have no tape. S1902846009 carries 129 + 109 early-voting provisionals and 50 Republican limited ballots, also with no tape.

Filing errors in the tape ZIPs

  • "EEC#271 Sunrise-McMillian Elementary School" in the Republican ZIP is the Democratic tape (172 ballots), and "EEC#272" in the Democratic ZIP is the Republican tape (11 ballots).
  • "EEC#156 Foster Village Elementary School" is the tape for Foster Elementary School (S1902547608, 52 ballots); Foster Village's own tape is EEC#158.
  • "Northpark YMCA.tif" (early voting) is a TIFF written without its image directory, so ordinary viewers cannot open it. Its pixels are intact; read as 490-pixel-wide raw grayscale, it is S1902993409 with 6,479 ballots, matching the CVR.
  • Duplicate images: "Grapevine Public Library 2.tif" and "City of Forest Hill City Hall 2.tif" (early voting) repeat the first scanner's tape; "EEC#149 Elks Lodge" and "EEC#621 Tanglewood" each contain the same report twice. "Limted 2.tif" is an empty tape from S1902850409, a serial with no CVRs.

Because about 11% of in-person ballots (37,077 of 327,732) were counted on scanners with no published tape, a tape-to-CVR comparison cannot independently confirm those ballots. Requesting the missing tapes, or the poll-closing reports for those units, would close the gap.

Roster redactions and ballot secrecy

Under countywide polling, a ballot is identifiable when it is the only one of its style in a group anyone can see. The CVR Report PDFs publish each ballot's precinct, party, voting method and polling place.

The April full roster undoes the redactions. In the "Election Roster_Redacted" files only 45 Republican and 26 Democratic rows are redacted. Of the precincts with one or two ballots of a party by a method, the full roster names voters in 106 of 108 (Republican mail), 98 of 100 (Democratic mail), 14 of 28 and 5 of 17 (early), and 22 of 35 and 19 of 29 (Election Day). In 133 of these groups exactly one ballot was cast and the voter is named, so that voter's selections can be read from the CVR: 57 Republican mail, 51 Democratic mail, 3 + 1 early, 10 + 11 Election Day.
Polling-place uniqueness was not redacted. 16,031 in-person ballots (Republican: 2,059 EV, 3,598 ED; Democratic: 3,820 EV, 6,554 ED) were the only ballot of their precinct and party cast at that vote center. The rosters do not carry a polling place, so these are not identifiable from the rosters alone, but anyone who knows where a voter voted (poll watchers, election workers, a sign-in list, or the voter's own account) can find the ballot.
